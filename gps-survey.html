Common Questions

Everything you need to know about our GPS surveying process

Is the GPS survey really included in the price?

Yes — 100%. The cost of GPS surveying is built into the purchase price of every plot we list. You will never receive a separate invoice for surveying. It is part of our standard offering to every buyer.

Who carries out the surveys?

All surveys are conducted by surveyors licensed and certified by the Ghana Institution of Surveyors (GhIS). We do not use unlicensed or informal surveyors under any circumstances.

How accurate is the GPS measurement?

Our surveying equipment achieves sub-metre accuracy — typically within ±0.5 metres. This far exceeds the standard required by the Lands Commission and is precise enough for any construction or legal purpose.

Is the survey plan registered with the government?

Yes. Every survey plan is lodged with and registered at the Lands Commission of Ghana. This provides a permanent government record of your plot boundaries that can be referenced in any legal proceeding.

Can I use the survey plan for my building permit?

Absolutely. Our certified survey plan is accepted by local assemblies and building authorities across Ghana as part of a building permit application. Your architect can also use it directly for site planning.
