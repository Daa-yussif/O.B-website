Common Questions

Everything you need to know about our payment plans

Is there really zero interest on all plans?

Yes — absolutely. All three of our plans (6, 12, and 24 months) carry zero interest. The price you see is the price you pay, split evenly across your chosen duration. No hidden charges, no surprises.

What deposit is required to start?

A minimum deposit of 10% of the plot price is required to secure your land and begin your instalment plan. We recommend 20–30% to reduce your monthly payments. Full deposits are also welcome.

Can I switch plans after I've started?

Yes, you can upgrade to a shorter plan at any time (e.g., move from 24 months to 12 months) by paying off more of the balance. Downgrading requires agent approval and may depend on your payment history.

When do I receive my title deed?

The full title deed is transferred to you upon completion of your final instalment. However, you receive a signed and notarised purchase agreement immediately after your deposit, which legally protects your ownership rights throughout the payment period.

Do you work with banks or mortgage providers?

Yes. We have partnerships with several Ghanaian banks and mortgage institutions. Our agents can connect you with the right provider based on your financial situation and preferred plot.
